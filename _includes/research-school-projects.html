Research & Explorations

  • BiDexHand

    Open-source, 16-DoF dexterous hand

    I've always been fascinated by the dexterity of the human hand. I designed and built BiDexHand across mechanics, electronics, and controls, using cable-driven fingers to explore low-cost robotic dexterity.

    Dexterous manipulation Cable-driven actuators ROS 2

  • LeFranX

    Imitation learning with Franka and XHand

    Imitation learning lets robots acquire motor skills from demonstrations. In this project, I explored ACT and diffusion policies and built a LeRobot-based framework for training and deploying visuomotor policies on a Franka FER arm and XHand.

    Imitation learning ACT / Diffusion Teleoperation

  • B.D. Bach

    BiDexHand plays Bach

    As a lifelong piano player, I wanted to build a robot that could share the keyboard with me. I taught BiDexHand to interpret MIDI scores and play Baroque music, starting with Bach's Well-Tempered Klavier.

    Dexterous manipulation Kinematic control C++

  • Impedance Control

    Contact and compliance in MuJoCo

    I use MuJoCo to compare impedance-control strategies and explore how they affect compliance and manipulation during contact.

    MuJoCo Impedance control Python

  • Apex Putter

    Robotic mini-golf

    Love a game of putt-putt? I combined computer vision, machine learning, and kinematic control to teach a Franka FER robot to line up and take a shot.

    ROS 2 Computer vision Machine learning
